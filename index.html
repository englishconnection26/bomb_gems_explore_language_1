💎 How to Play Vocab Gem Quest

🎯 Goal

  • Collect the most points by flipping cards and finding gems!

👥 Setup

  • Choose 2–4 teams and give each one a name.
  • Pick how many bomb cards (out of 26) to include.

🎮 Playing

  • The card grid starts locked for each team's turn.
  • The host clicks "❓ Ask Question" to show a question — it auto-closes after 20 seconds, or the host can close it early.
  • ✅ Answer correctly and the card grid unlocks — the team picks one card!
  • ❌ Answer wrong (or run out of time) and the grid stays locked — the host can ask another question to try again.
  • 💎 A gem card adds points to that team's score.
  • 💣 A bomb card subtracts points instead!
  • After a card is flipped, play passes to the next team, and their grid locks again until they answer a question correctly.

🏆 Winning

  • Once every card is flipped, the game ends and the team with the highest score wins!
